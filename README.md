## 📽️Favorite Movie

### A Silent Voice (Koe no Katachi)

**Genre:** Drama & Romance
**Studio:** Kyoto Animation

**A Silent Voice**, or **Koe no Katachi**, is a Japanese animated film that follows the life of a deaf girl, Shouka Nishimiya and her bully Shoya Ishida. The film starts with Ishida relentlessly bullying Nishimiya due to her singularity among the students because of her condition. It ultimately stopped when Ishida pulled and damaged Nishimiya's hearing aid, causing her to move out of the school.

For the coming years under college, Ishida developed full regret for his actions as a child, causing his mental health to deteriorate, leading to planning on taking his own life. Fortunately, it was prevented when his mother discovered Ishida's plans. One day he stumbled upon a grown-up Nishimiya in a sign language class. When their eyes met, Nishimiya panicked and ran away from him. Ishida ultimately caught up to her and showed that he learned how to communicate with sign language. He then asked her if they can be friends, which began their path towards healing.

## 🎬 Favorite Series

### Violet Evergarden

**Genre:** Drama, Fantasy & Coming-of-age
**Studio:** Kyoto Animation

**Violet Evergarden** is a Japanese animated TV series based on its light novel version. It starts with an orphan named Violet, she was taken care of by a military officer named Gilbert Bougainvillea. During her time under Gilbert, she was raised as a soldier in the Great War. Violet was seen as a human weapon used for war, causing her to become emotionless.One day in a brutal conflict on the battlefield, Violet and Gilbert were left in a dangerous situation, Violet had lost both her arms, while Gilbert was immobilized.

On his final moment, Gilbert has confessed his feelings towards Violet by saying, "I love you." Violet woke up on the hospital bed with the war coming to an end, she immediately searched for Gilbert, ingrained in her mind the final words he uttered. Being an emotionless ex-soldier, broken by the war, she cannot understand what those words mean.

In search for answers, she took a job as an "Auto-Memory Doll," a ghostwriter for people who transcribe the feelings of their clients into heartfelt letters, trying to gain her emotions back and understand Gilbert's feelings.
